Biomedical Interface
Clinical Impact

Assistive Technology

Designed for patients suffering from severe motor impairment or locked-in syndromes, this technology provides a vital lifeline. By translating ocular and electrophysiological signals into actionable automation routines, it restores autonomy and peace of mind for patients and their families.

Explore More Achievements

Core System Capabilities

Ocular Control

Hands-Free Environmental Control

Patients can independently adjust automated hospital bed inclines for comfort and operate household appliances such as lighting, fans, and televisions using subtle, intuitive eye movements and blinks.

Telemetry & Telehealth

Continuous Safety Monitoring

Continuously monitors patient heart rate and vitals. Upon detecting anomalous biometric readings or distress thresholds, the system automatically triggers an emergency SMS notification to caregivers and doctors.

Feedback Loop

Auditory Voice Confirmation

Equipped with an integrated speech synthesizer that verbally confirms command reception in real time, preventing accidental activations and giving users clear acoustic feedback.

Emergency Response

Instant Distress Beacon

A quick series of intentional ocular blinks activates an immediate priority alert dispatch to designated guardians, ensuring critical help is reachable without physical exertion.
